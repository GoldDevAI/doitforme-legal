Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoItForMe ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application provided by GoldDev ("we", "us", or "our").

DoItForMe is a local marketplace that helps users publish and discover errands and tasks offered in their area.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • User ID associated with your account
  • Profile name
  • Tasks and errands you publish or manage
  • Photos you choose to upload with a task
  • Precise location, when you grant location permission
  • Basic app usage and technical diagnostics, if enabled through our service providers

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Publish, discover, display, and manage local tasks and errands
  • Show relevant tasks on the map and calculate their distance
  • Store and display task photos you choose to upload
  • Maintain app security, reliability, and functionality
  • Fix bugs and improve the product

3. Authentication and Guest Use

An account is required to publish and manage tasks and to use other account-based features of the App.

4. Third-Party Services

DoItForMe uses third-party services to operate the App. These providers may process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Expo: https://expo.dev/privacy

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

The App may use technical analytics or diagnostics tools to understand app stability, feature usage, and overall product performance. We do not use your data for third-party advertising tracking.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Task, photo, profile, and related account data may remain stored until you delete your account or request deletion where applicable.

7. Account Deletion

DoItForMe provides in-app account deletion from the profile screen: Profilo > Elimina account. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing doitformesupport@gmail.com. When you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize relevant personal data associated with that account, unless we must retain certain information for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate the App
  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud and abuse

9. Children’s Privacy

DoItForMe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove the data if appropriate.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information processed through the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, or objection to certain processing. You can contact us using the email below for privacy-related requests.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date.
