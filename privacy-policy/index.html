Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoItForMe ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application provided by GoldDev ("we", "us", or "our").

DoItForMe is a local marketplace that helps users publish and discover errands and tasks offered in their area.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • User ID associated with your account
  • Profile name
  • Tasks and errands you publish or manage
  • Photos you choose to upload with a task
  • Applications, offers, task status, and reviews
  • Messages exchanged with the other participant in an assigned task
  • Precise location, when you grant location permission; other users only see an approximate area of a task until it is assigned
  • Before and After work photos, visible only to the poster and the helper
  • Text you type to search for an address
  • Push notification token, notification preferences and alert settings (radius, categories, minimum amount)
  • Reports, blocks and moderation decisions
  • Payment records: amounts, fees, tips, status and dates of each payment, refund and transfer, the identifiers of the related Stripe operations, and notes written by our support team when a case is handled manually
  • Whether your payout account with Stripe is ready to receive payments (the identity, bank and card details themselves are collected and held by Stripe, not by us)
  • Cancellations and withdrawals of tasks after assignment, with their date, cause and whether the work had started
  • Points, levels, badges and referral codes
  • Basic app usage and technical diagnostics, if enabled through our service providers

The list of your recent task searches is stored only on your device; DoItForMe does not store that history on its servers. You can clear it from the search screen, and it is cleared when you sign out.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Publish, discover, display, and manage local tasks and errands
  • Show relevant tasks on the map and calculate their distance
  • Store and display task photos you choose to upload
  • Coordinate assigned tasks through private task-bound chat
  • Show task history and reviews to the relevant users
  • Maintain app security, reliability, and functionality
  • Fix bugs and improve the product
  • Process payments, refunds and transfers, handle payment problems and disputes, and keep the accounting and tax records we are required to keep
  • Review repeated cancellations and withdrawals after assignment, and attempts to move payments outside the App, to prevent abuse. Any warning or suspension is decided by a person, not automatically

3. Authentication and Guest Use

An account is required to publish and manage tasks and to use other account-based features of the App.

Without an account you can browse open tasks in the App (Discover, search, map and task details) and open a task from a link someone shared with you. To apply, save, report, chat or publish you need an account.

Open tasks are public: their title, description, photos, category, price and approximate area, together with the poster's short name (first name and last initial) and level, are visible to anyone using the App, even without an account, and to anyone who opens the task's link. The exact address and your contact details are never shown.

The same open tasks, with the same limits, are also shown on the DoItForMe website, which anyone can browse without an account. Task pages are marked so that search engines do not index them.

4. Third-Party Services

DoItForMe uses third-party services to operate the App. These providers may process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Expo (app updates and push notifications): https://expo.dev/privacy
  • Railway (backend hosting): https://railway.com/legal/privacy
  • Stripe (payments and payout accounts): https://stripe.com/privacy
  • Vercel (website hosting and website analytics): https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Maps Platform (address search and in-app maps): https://policies.google.com/privacy

Payments: payments made in the App are processed by Stripe, which acts as an independent payment provider for card and payout data. DoItForMe does not see or store full card numbers or bank account details. To receive payments, helpers complete an identity and bank verification directly with Stripe. If you dispute a charge with your bank, we may share the information about the task that Stripe and the bank need to examine the dispute. Payments made outside the App are not processed by us.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

The App may use technical analytics or diagnostics tools to understand app stability, feature usage, and overall product performance. We do not use your data for third-party advertising tracking.

On the website we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors: it records aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring site, the country and the type of device and browser. The public pages of the website do not set cookies of their own; the only cookies we use are the technical ones needed to sign in to the staff back office, plus any technical cookie our hosting provider may set to protect the website from abuse.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Task, photo, profile, and related account data may remain stored until you delete your account or request deletion where applicable.

Chat remains available while an assigned task is active. When the task is completed or cancelled, the chat becomes read-only and is retained for 30 days from closure. After that period, the conversation, messages, participant links, and related chat events are deleted. Minimum task history and valid reviews are managed separately and are not removed by the chat deletion process. Payment records are kept for the period required by accounting and tax law, even after you delete your account, and are limited to what we need for that purpose. Counts of cancellations and withdrawals are derived from task history and follow its retention.

7. Account Deletion

DoItForMe provides in-app account deletion from the profile screen: Profilo > Elimina account. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing doitformesupport@gmail.com. When you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize relevant personal data associated with that account, unless we must retain certain information for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate the App
  • With Stripe and your bank, to process payments and handle disputes
  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud and abuse

9. Children’s Privacy

DoItForMe is intended for users aged 18 or older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove the data if appropriate.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information processed through the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, or objection to certain processing. You can contact us using the email below for privacy-related requests.
